Clinician FAQ

Questions, answered.

Open a question for the short version. We can walk through the details with you.

Ask us in a walkthrough
What can I upload?

You can add health PDFs, TXT files, and supported CSV or TSV files. n1 also supports selected raw gene files.

What happens after I upload a file?

n1 checks each file, reads the content, and sorts each item by type and date. Every result keeps a link to its source.

Can I check each result against the original file?

Yes. Open the file, page, date, and range behind a result. You can correct the record before you use it in a report.

Can I make a custom report?

Yes. Start with a built-in report or describe the report you need. Choose the reader, records, units, and detail before n1 makes the draft.

Who approves the final report?

You do. Every report starts as a draft. A clinician checks the sources, edits the text, and approves the version that can be shared.

How do patient invites work?

A doctor can create a patient space and invite the patient to claim it. A patient can also ask a doctor to connect. The link only turns on after the invite is accepted.

How is patient data protected?

Files use encryption when they move and while they are stored. You can hide names, stop source files from being stored, or pick a storage region. See the controls →

Can I try n1 before I sign up?

Yes. Try the live demo with no sign-up, start a free trial, or book a walkthrough.
