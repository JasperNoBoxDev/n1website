How we build

You can test each pledge.

01

The source stays attached

Why
A finding is difficult to review when you cannot see where it came from.
In n1
Each finding we pull out keeps a link back to its original source page.
Review the provenance example →
02

Drafts stay with the doctor

Why
Sorted data is not the same as a clinical call.
In n1
The clinician checks the source pages, edits the report, and chooses what is shared.
Inspect the review states →
03

Time stays visible

Why
The latest result can hide how fast, and in which direction, things moved.
In n1
Dates stay with each value, so you can review change alongside context.
Open the health-over-time example →
04

The scope stays explicit

Why
An AI-generated interpretation is not a reviewed clinical conclusion.
In n1
n1 can draft notes on what results may mean, but a doctor must check, edit, and decide how to use them.
Review the report labels →
